# Xiaohongshu product-demo defaults

## Deliverables

| Asset | Default | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Primary video | 1080x1440, 24 fps, H.264, muted | Feed post |
| Alternate video | 1080x1920 | Full-screen vertical placement |
| Poster | First clean rendered frame | Cover or fallback thumbnail |

Use MP4 for the final deliverable so UI text, gradients, and motion stay crisp.

## Composition

- Reserve roughly 280–340 px at the top of a 1080x1440 canvas for copy.
- Use a small eyebrow label, a strong benefit headline, and a quiet support line.
- Present a landscape recording in a thin browser card that follows the source aspect ratio. Use a phone card only for portrait recordings.
- Keep ornamental shapes large, soft, and low contrast. Do not use scattered confetti.
- A mascot can overlap an empty corner but must not cover the demonstrated action.

## Review

- Read every line at phone-preview size.
- Confirm there is no personal data, account identifier, notification, API key, or private document in the recording.
- Confirm the pointer or touch action is understandable without narration.
- Confirm the first frame explains the benefit and the last frame loops without a distracting jump.
- Confirm the product card has no black letterbox, thick device bezel, or accidental stretch.
